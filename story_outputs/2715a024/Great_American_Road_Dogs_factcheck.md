## 1. Bud Across America

**Researcher context:**

> In 1903 Horatio Nelson Jackson and mechanic Sewall Crocker made the first automobile crossing of the United States, San Francisco to New York. Partway through, in Idaho, they bought a pit bull named Bud, who rode with them wearing goggles against the alkali dust. Photographs are held by the Smithsonian National Museum of American History.

**Sources the model cited (unverified):**

- Smithsonian National Museum of American History (artifact and photograph collection)
- Dayton Duncan and Ken Burns, Horatio's Drive: America's First Road Trip (2003)
